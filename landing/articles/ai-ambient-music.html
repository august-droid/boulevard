What ambient music is

Ambient music is built to be as ignorable as it is listenable. It has texture and atmosphere but little rhythm or melody to grab you. The whole point, since Brian Eno coined the term, is music that colors a space and then recedes. It is the natural soundtrack for focus, sleep, meditation, and calm.
