Reading and music compete for the same part of your brain. Here is the kind of AI music that supports reading instead, and how to start it free.

Quick answer

The best music for reading is quiet, wordless, and even, because lyrics compete directly with the language part of your brain you read with. Boulevard is an AI music app that generates wordless, low-key tracks: tap Focus or Chill and read. Free, no ads. Boulevard is the AI alternative to Spotify.

Reading and lyrics fight for the same brain

Reading is a language task. So are lyrics. Play a song with words while you read and the two compete, which is why a paragraph sometimes has to be read twice. The fix is not silence, which many people find distracting. The fix is music with no words.

Why AI music suits reading

  • Low dynamics. Nothing jumps and pulls you off the page.
  • It does not end. A long reading session gets a continuous, seamless background.
