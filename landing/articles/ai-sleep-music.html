Why a sleep playlist works against you

The danger with a normal playlist at bedtime is contrast. A quiet track ends, a louder one starts, and you are awake again. An ad plays. A song you have a memory attached to makes your brain start narrating. Sleep music has to do the opposite of what most music is built to do: it has to be forgettable.

Why AI music is good at sleep

  • No volume spikes. Tracks are generated to a calm target, so nothing jumps.
  • No lyrics keeping your mind awake. Sleep tracks stay wordless and slow.
  • It keeps going. No playlist ending in the middle of the night with sudden silence.
