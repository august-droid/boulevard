HOW-TO

How to cancel Spotify Premium. Step by step.

Canceling Spotify Premium takes about two minutes, but you cannot do it from inside the app. Here is the step-by-step for every device, plus how to keep your playlists.

By August Tange, Founder, Boulevard Published May 17, 2026 Updated May 17, 2026 5 min read

Quick answer

To cancel Spotify Premium, go to spotify.com/account in a web browser, open Manage your plan, and select Cancel Premium. You cannot cancel from inside the Spotify app. Your account stays Premium until the end of the current billing period, then drops to the free tier. Your playlists and saved music are not deleted.

Where to cancel, by how you signed up

The single most common reason people cannot find the cancel button: they are looking in the wrong place. Where you cancel depends on who bills you.

Through Apple (App Store)iPhone Settings, then Subscriptions
Through Google PlayPlay Store, then Payments & subscriptions
Bundled (carrier or other deal)The provider that bills you

Not sure which applies to you? Check your bank or App Store receipt. The merchant name on the charge tells you who to cancel with.

Before you cancel: back up your playlists

Canceling Premium does not delete your library, but if you ever close the account entirely, it is gone. Two minutes of insurance:

  1. Go to your privacy settings on the Spotify account page and request your data. The download includes a list of your playlists.
  2. Or use a free playlist-transfer tool to copy playlists to wherever you go next.
  3. Note the two or three playlists you actually use. Most people only need those.

How to cancel on the web (works for everyone)

  1. Open a browser and go to spotify.com. Log in.
  2. Click your profile, then Account.
  3. Select Manage your plan.
  4. Scroll to Cancel Premium (sometimes under Change plan, then Cancel Spotify Premium).
  5. Confirm. You will get an email receipt and a cancellation date.

This is the only method that works no matter how you signed up.

How to cancel on iPhone

It depends on how you pay:

  • If you subscribed through Apple (you see Spotify under Apple's subscriptions): open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap Spotify, then Cancel Subscription.
  • If you subscribed directly with Spotify: use the web steps above. The iOS app cannot cancel a Spotify-billed plan.

How to cancel on Android

  • If you subscribed through Google Play: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile, tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select Spotify, and tap Cancel subscription.
  • If you subscribed directly with Spotify: use the web steps above.

What happens after you cancel

  • You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for. Spotify does not refund the remainder.
  • After that, your account becomes Spotify Free: audio ads, limited skips on mobile, no offline play.
  • Your playlists, liked songs, and follows stay on the account.
  • You can resubscribe any time.

What to use instead

Most people cancel Spotify for one of two reasons: the $11.99 price, or stale recommendations. Where you go next depends on your listening:

  • For specific songs and famous artists: Apple Music or YouTube Music are direct replacements.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel Spotify Premium?
Go to spotify.com/account in a web browser, open Manage your plan, and select Cancel Premium. You cannot cancel from inside the Spotify app. If you subscribed through Apple or Google, cancel in that platform's subscription settings instead.
Will I lose my playlists if I cancel Spotify?
No. Canceling Premium drops you to the free tier but keeps your playlists, liked songs, and follows. You only lose your library if you fully close the account. Back up playlists first to be safe.
Do I get a refund when I cancel Spotify?
No. Spotify does not refund the unused part of a billing period. You keep Premium until the period you already paid for ends, then the account becomes Spotify Free.
Why can't I cancel Spotify in the app?
Spotify does not allow plan cancellation inside its mobile app. You must use a web browser at spotify.com, or, if you were billed through Apple or Google, cancel in that store's subscription settings.
What is the best app to use after canceling Spotify?
For specific songs, Apple Music or YouTube Music. For background, focus, and sleep listening, Boulevard, the free AI alternative to Spotify. Many people install Boulevard before canceling so the switch is not cold.
