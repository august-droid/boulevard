Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026 · Last updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Boulevard ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Boulevard mobile application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information. If you sign up via Apple, Google, or Facebook, we receive a user identifier (and, where you choose to share it, an email address) from the identity provider.
  • Anonymous device identifiers. Before you sign up, we generate a random local identifier so we can save your taste profile, library, and listening history on your device.
  • Listening activity. Songs played, skipped, completed, saved, and shared, plus the listen duration for each event. We use this to power recommendations.
  • App interaction events. Vibe selections, paywall views, and similar in-app actions used to improve the Service.
  • Subscription information. If you purchase a premium subscription, we receive a transaction identifier from the Google Play billing system. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Diagnostic information. Anonymized crash reports and performance metrics. No personally identifiable information is attached.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Service — playing music, syncing your library across sessions, and personalizing recommendations to your taste.
  • To enforce daily listening limits on free accounts and to manage premium entitlements.
  • To improve content quality. Aggregated, non-identifying listening trends inform which songs we promote, retire, or generate next.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse of the Service.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the following service providers, who process it solely on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • Cloudflare — audio streaming and content delivery.
  • Google (Play Billing) — processes subscription purchases.
  • Apple, Google, Facebook — if you choose to sign in using one of these providers, the provider verifies your identity to us.

We may disclose information when legally required (e.g., subpoena, court order) or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Boulevard or its users.

4. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account exists. Listening activity is retained for as long as needed to operate the recommendation system. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing the address below; we will delete the data within 30 days unless retention is legally required.

5. Children

Boulevard is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest for stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Transfers

Our service providers operate in the United States and may process your information there. By using Boulevard you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
